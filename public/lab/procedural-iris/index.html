Procedural Iris

Eye, lids, skin, lashes and brow, all computed from formulas. Every variable is a slider; every formula is editable GLSL.

Hand controlFront camera · tracking runs on this device
Hand control starts when the page loads.

Starter mapping. Right hand: move it and the eye follows · open hand ↔ fist opens and closes the lids · thumb–index pinch sets the pupil · tilt shifts iris color blue → brown · move closer to zoom. Left hand: move it to move the light · open sets lash length · pinch sets fiber count.
Learn pose: set the sliders to a look, hold a hand pose, tap Learn pose. With two or more learned poses, your hands blend between them and every parameter moves at once.

Formulas (operators)

Each box is one GLSL expression dropped into the shader. Use any P_… uniform shown beside a slider, the locals listed, and helpers pfbm(p, period), fbm(p), pnoise(p, period), voro(p) → vec2(F₁, F₂), hash(p). Write floats with a decimal point (2.0, not 2).
