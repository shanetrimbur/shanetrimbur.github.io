SHANE’S LAB / EXPERIMENT 001

Your hand.
Their fight.

Four fingers. Four limbs. Your wrist moves the body.

  1. Hold the phone in one hand. Show your other hand to the rear camera.
  2. Hold a relaxed open hand still to calibrate. Move toward your opponent to punch; pull back to retract.
  3. Index/middle bend and extend the arms. Curl ring/little to lift a leg, then push toward your opponent to kick. Drag your screen thumb to move.

Camera processing stays on your device. Tracking files download on first use. This is a controls experiment, not a biomechanics simulation.

Controls & credits

Move right to punch from the left fighter; move left from the right fighter. Index and middle fingers independently bend/extend arms; spread them to swing elbows. Curl ring or little finger to raise that leg, then move toward the opponent to kick. Wrist tilt leans the torso, thumb spread twists it, lowering your hand bends the knees. One leg stays grounded. Tracking loss returns to guard.

Camera mode: drag anywhere in the arena to move. Touch practice: arena drag strikes; the lower thumb strip moves. Keyboard: 1–4 select a practice limb, A/D move left/right, G guards, J strikes, ↑/↓ aim. Menu lets you choose either fighter. Mirroring affects both camera preview and controls.
