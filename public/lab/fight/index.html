SHANE’S LAB / EXPERIMENT 001

Your hand.
Their fight.

Control a grounded anatomy fighter with the hand behind your phone.

  1. Hold the phone in one hand. Show your other hand to the rear camera.
  2. Hold still to calibrate. Move sideways to extend, up or down to aim.
  3. Use your thumb to select a limb, step, or guard.

Camera processing stays on your device. Tracking files download on first use. This is a controls experiment, not a biomechanics simulation.

Controls & credits

Move your hand sideways from neutral to extend; return to center to recover. Move vertically to aim. Keep the whole hand visible. Tracking loss returns you to guard.

Touch practice: drag across the arena. Keyboard: 1/2/3 select a limb, A/D step, G guards, J strikes, ↑/↓ aim.
